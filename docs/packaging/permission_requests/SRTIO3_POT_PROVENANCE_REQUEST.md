# SrTiO3 POT provenance and redistribution request

To: generator/owner of the six SrTiO3 pair-potential files and any contributing
data rights holders

The proposed distribution currently bundles `O-O`, `O-Sr`, `O-Ti`, `Sr-Sr`,
`Sr-Ti`, and `Ti-Ti` POT files copied exactly from QLIP revision
`a619ab379c62b5edefd6bb00076267e149f283ce`. Git history shows that the files
were introduced together by commit `6aed49df639722a1fcfe5b991d1e90551482a5d5`,
but the files and commit do not record their generator inputs.

Please provide or confirm:

1. who generated and owns the files;
2. generator software, version, parameters, and creation date;
3. every source dataset, structure corpus, fitted parameter source, or other
   third-party input;
4. the licence/terms for those inputs and authority to redistribute the output;
5. permission to distribute the six exact files in an MIT-licensed repository;
   and
6. required notices and scientific citations.

If permission is denied or cannot be established, the files must be removed
and the public workflow must require a user-supplied POT root. No permission is
inferred solely from their presence in the QLIP repository.
